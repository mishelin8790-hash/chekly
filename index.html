FAQ

Where is my data?
Only on your iPhone, and in your private iCloud if you enable sync. See the Privacy Policy.
How do I restore my Pro purchase?
Settings → Go Pro → Restore Purchases.
How do I cancel my subscription?
iPhone Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions → Chekly.
How do I move data to a new phone?
Turn on iCloud sync, or Settings → Create Backup and open the file on the new phone.
Import from "My Expenses" or a bank statement?
Settings → Import from CSV.
